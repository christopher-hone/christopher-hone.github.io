Papers in preparation:

1. Mod two perverse sheaves on the real affine Grassmannian in type A.
This is using real geometric extensions to describe the tensor category of perverse sheaves on the real affine Grassmannian. The main result is the construction of a fully faithful exact monoidal functor from the mod two algebraic representations of the general linear group to this category of real perverse sheaves.

2. Equivariant topological K theory at an automorphism.
Given a finite group G with automorphism s, one has an s-invariant character ring, a free module over the cyclotomic integers with orthonormal basis the s-invariant irreducible reps of G (eg, Glauberman correspondence). In this work I construct a 2-periodic fully commutative genuine equivariant ring spectrum which recovers this Mackey functor as its homotopy groups, with Chern character recovering the twisted character map of the s-invariant characters.

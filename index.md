## Chris' website

I am a postdoc at the GEOTOP centre within the University of Copenhagen since October 2025. I'm interested in geometric representation theory, algebraic geometry, and the representation theory of finite groups. 

To contact me, please use christopher(dot)t(dot)hone(at)gmail(dot)com.

Papers:

1. [The geometry of tilting composition series via Richardson varieties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16937)(2026), joint with Joe Baine.
2. [Semisimplifying categorical Heisenberg actions and periodic equivalences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07377)(2025), joint with Finn Klein, Bregje Pauwels, Alexander Sherman, Oded Yacobi, Victor L. Zhang.
3. [Geometric extensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.11780)(2025), joint with Geordie Williamson.

Papers in preparation:

1. Real geometric extensions.
This is essentially the content of the second chapter of my PhD thesis.

2. Mod two perverse sheaves on the real affine Grassmannian in type A.
This is using real geometric extensions to describe the tensor category of perverse sheaves on the real affine Grassmannian. The main result is the construction of a fully faithful exact monoidal functor from the mod two algebraic representations of the general linear group to this category of real perverse sheaves.
